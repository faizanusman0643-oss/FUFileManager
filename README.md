# FU Workshop Alert

AIOU (Aaghi LMS) workshop and quiz alarm app for Android. Prince Faizan · FU · Innovating Tomorrow.

**Download APK:** Releases → `workshop-alert-latest` → `FUWorkshopAlert.apk`

- Alarms: 1 day before (8 PM), workshop morning (8 AM), X minutes before start, at start, when the quiz opens (5th day), and on the quiz's last day
- Runs in the background: alarms are re-set automatically after a phone restart
- Paste or share an AIOU SMS or Teams link and the date, time and link fill in automatically
- Join Teams button and Aaghi LMS shortcut
- Data is saved on the phone, with Export/Import backup
